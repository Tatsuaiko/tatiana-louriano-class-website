My Time at an NRG Night Market

Back in October 2024, NRG invited my Valorant team, along with a bunch of other people, teams, and content creators to their Night Market event in LA. It somehow ended up fulfilling a part of my life that I had no idea needed filling. I saw many creators I knew and was fortunate enough to talk with and have a good laugh with them. I met creators such as Mooda, Ethos, Sapnap, S0M, FNS, and Tokibbi!

  • I was gifted an NRG jersey from the team for being a fan.
  • I talked about Valorant with Mooda and Ethos.
  • There was amazing Asian cuisine being it was themed an Asian Night Market.
  • Posed with a few Valorant weapon skin props and life-sized video game characters.
"When it comes to Valorant, focus on consistency and playing often." — FNS (2024)

Fun fact: I've been a one trick viper main since I began playing Valorant!
